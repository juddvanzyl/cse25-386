OUR STORY

696 DEPT is more than a brand. It's a statement.Inspired by street culture, built for creatives and dreamers. We create pieces that speak loud without saying a word.


MISSION

To build timeless streetwear that empowers self expression and connects culture. We strive to create clothing that tells a story, inspires confidence, and brings people together through shared identity and creative energy. Every stitch, every graphic, and every piece is designed with purpose to make you feel seen, heard, and unforgettable. At 696 DEPT, we don't just follow trends; we set them. We are building a movement where fashion meets freedom, and culture meets courage.
